## Section PG-DMT-1: First Registration of a Motor Vehicle (Part I of the Motor Traffic Act)
### PG-DMT-1.1: Statutory Requirement for Registration
Under Section 2 of the Motor Traffic Act (Cap. 203), no person shall possess or use a motor vehicle in Sri Lanka unless that vehicle is registered with the Commissioner-General of Motor Traffic and carries valid identification plates and a Certificate of Registration (CR / Vehicle Book).

### PG-DMT-1.2: Required Documents for New / Imported Vehicle Registration
1. Completed Application Form **M.T.A. 2** (Application for Registration of a Motor Vehicle).
2. Original Sri Lanka Customs Goods Declaration (**CUSDEC**) and Customs Assessment Notice verifying payment of import duties and excise levies.
3. Original commercial invoice, Bill of Lading, and De-registration / Export Certificate from the country of origin (for reconditioned vehicles).
4. Certificate of Inspection / Prototype Approval and weight certificate issued by a DMT Examiner of Motor Vehicles (confirming compliance with dimensions under Gazette Extraordinary No. 1847/32).
5. Certified copy of the applicant's National Identity Card (NIC), Driving Licence, or Valid Passport, along with two certified studio photographs (2.0 x 2.5 inches).
6. If the vehicle is subject to a hire-purchase, lease, or mortgage facility, Form **M.T.A. 3** signed by the financial institution to register the **Absolute Owner**.
7. Payment of the prescribed first-registration fee and number-plate fee as scheduled under **Gazette Extraordinary No. 2306/02 (2022)**.

## Section PG-DMT-2: Transfer of Vehicle Ownership (Section 12 – 14 of the Motor Traffic Act)
### PG-DMT-2.1: Statutory Deadlines for Buyer and Seller
1. **Seller (Current Registered Owner) Obligation:** Within **fourteen (14) days** of transferring possession of a motor vehicle, the seller must notify the Commissioner-General of Motor Traffic using **Form M.T.A. 6 (Copy A)** via registered post or in person, retaining **Copy A1** and official receipt **C.M.T. 52** as proof of divestiture.
2. **Buyer (New Owner) Obligation:** Within **fourteen (14) days** of acquiring ownership of a motor vehicle, the buyer must apply to the DMT to register the transfer of ownership. Failure to submit transfer documents within 14 days incurs statutory late penalty fees per day/month of delay as scheduled in Gazette No. 2306/02.

### PG-DMT-2.2: Mandatory Document Checklist ("The Transfer Set")
1. **Form M.T.A. 6 (Copies B and B1):** Signed by the seller (transferor) and certified by a Justice of the Peace (JP), Grama Niladhari, or Attorney-at-Law.
2. **Form M.T.A. 8 (Copies C and C1):** Application for registration of the new owner upon transfer of possession, duly completed and signed by the buyer (transferee).
3. **Original Certificate of Registration (CR / Vehicle Book):** Mandatory for cancellation/endorsement.
4. **Valid Revenue Licence:** Original and photocopy of the current year's Revenue Licence for the vehicle.
5. **Identity Verification:** Photocopies of the National Identity Cards (NIC) of both the seller and the buyer, certified by a JP or Grama Niladhari.
6. **Photographs of the Buyer:** Two colour or black-and-white studio photographs (2.0 x 2.5 inches), with one certified on the reverse by a Grama Niladhari, JP, or staff-grade officer Confirming identity.
7. **Deletion of Absolute Ownership (If Applicable):** If a leasing company or bank is listed as the Absolute Owner on the Certificate of Registration, an official **Letter of Deletion / No-Objection Letter** together with **Form M.T.A. 5** stamped by the financial institution is required.

### PG-DMT-2.3: Submission Channels (One-Day Service vs. Normal Service)
1. **One-Day Service:** Available at the DMT Head Office (Narahenpita) and designated branch offices for documents submitted during priority morning hours (typically 09:00 AM – 12:00 PM). Requires verification of seller/buyer identity and payment of the One-Day Service fee under Gazette No. 2306/02; the updated Certificate of Registration is issued on the same working day.
2. **Normal Service:** Documents may be submitted at DMT Narahenpita, DMT District Offices, or Divisional Secretariats. The applicant receives temporary receipt **C.M.T. 52** (retaining M.T.A. 6 Copy B1 and M.T.A. 8 Copy C1), and the new Certificate of Registration is dispatched via registered post.

## Section PG-DMT-3: Driving Licence Application, Renewal, and Foreign Conversion (Part VII)
### PG-DMT-3.1: New Driving Licence & Learner's Permit (Werahera & District Offices)
1. **Eligibility:** Minimum age of **17 years** to apply for a Learner's Permit (written test), and **18 years** to sit the practical driving trial for light vehicles/motorcycles (21 years for heavy vehicle classes).
2. **Medical Certificate:** Applicants must obtain a Certificate of Physical Fitness from the **National Transport Medical Institute (NTMI)**.
3. **Written Examination & Learner's Permit:** Upon passing the computer/written test on traffic signs (Gazette No. 1940/21 & 2128/09) and road rules, a Learner's Permit is issued valid for up to 18 months.
4. **Practical Trial:** After completing the mandatory minimum learning period (at least 3 months from the Learner's Permit issue date), the applicant sits the practical trial before a DMT Examiner.

### PG-DMT-3.2: Foreign Driving Licence Conversion & Recognition (DMT Circular 2022/14 & Gazette 2463/04)
1. Under **DMT Circular No. 2022/14** and **Gazette Extraordinary No. 2463/04 (2025)**, foreign nationals holding a valid foreign driving licence or International Driving Permit (IDP) must obtain temporary recognition/endorsement or convert their licence via the Department of Motor Traffic (Werahera / Airport Counter) upon paying the prescribed foreign licence levy and DMT fee.
2. Sri Lankan citizens returning with a valid foreign driving licence from an approved jurisdiction may apply for a direct conversion to a Sri Lankan Driving Licence of the equivalent vehicle class at DMT Werahera by submitting their original foreign licence, passport with arrival visa endorsements, NTMI medical certificate, and conversion fee under Gazette No. 2306/02.

## Section PG-DMT-4: Annual Revenue Licence, Emission Testing (VET), and Fitness Certificates
### PG-DMT-4.1: Mandatory Vehicular Exhaust Emission Test (VET)
1. Under the Motor Traffic (Emission Control) Regulations (**Gazette No. 1295/11**, as amended by **No. 1557/14, 1887/20, and 2083/03**), every motor vehicle (except exempted vintage or zero-emission electric vehicles) must obtain an annual **Vehicle Emission Test (VET) Certificate** from an accredited testing centre (Drivegreen or Laughs Eco Sri) prior to renewing its annual Revenue Licence at the Provincial Department of Motor Traffic or Divisional Secretariat.
2. Both petrol (idle/high-idle CO and HC limits) and diesel (free-acceleration smoke opacity k-factor limits) must fall within the statutory thresholds prescribed in Gazette No. 2083/03.

### PG-DMT-4.2: Certificate of Fitness (CF) for Commercial & Heavy Vehicles
Under Part IX of the Motor Traffic Act, omnibuses, lorries, commercial goods vehicles, motor tricycles used for hire (Gazette No. 2003/41), and school vans must undergo an annual physical inspection by a licensed Examiner of Motor Vehicles to obtain a **Certificate of Fitness (CF)** verifying braking efficiency, tyre tread depth, lighting, steering integrity, and chassis soundness before a Revenue Licence can be issued.

## Section PG-DMT-5: Approval for Vehicle Modifications & Structural Alterations (Section 19)
### PG-DMT-5.1: Prior Approval Requirement for Alterations
1. Under **Section 19** of the Motor Traffic Act and the Construction of Vehicles Regulations (**Gazette Extraordinary No. 1847/32**), a registered owner must not alter a vehicle's engine, fuel type, chassis structure, body colour, seating capacity, lighting assembly, or exhaust system in a manner that deviates from the particulars recorded on the Certificate of Registration or manufacturer specifications without **prior written inspection and approval from the Commissioner-General of Motor Traffic**.
2. **Procedure for Legal Modification (Engine / Colour / Fuel Conversion):**
   - Submit an application for alteration at the DMT Technical Branch (Werahera) prior to or immediately alongside the inspection request.
   - Present the original Certificate of Registration, purchase invoices/customs documents for any replacement engine or component, and an engineer's certificate where structural changes are involved.
   - Undergo physical inspection by a DMT Motor Vehicle Examiner to verify compliance with safety, lighting, dimension (Gazette No. 1847/32), and noise/emission standards (Gazette No. 924/12, 1738/37, and 2083/03).
   - Pay the prescribed alteration endorsement fee (Gazette No. 2306/02) to have the updated particulars officially printed on the Certificate of Registration.
